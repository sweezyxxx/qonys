## Approved project title

Development of an AI-Powered Residential Real Estate Visualization and Intelligent Property Selection Platform with 3D Site Visualization, Floor-Level Layouts, and Interactive Mapping

## Functional basis supplied by the stakeholder

These items come directly from the new project description. Their implementation details remain subject to the approvals below.

- One shared residential development catalogue covering different developers, with city selection and list/map modes.
- Three business roles: buyer, property developer and platform administrator.
- An administrator manually checks a developer representative and creates the developer account. The new flow replaces the previous self-registration and approval-application workflow.
- Development, building, floor and apartment navigation, using images with linked SVG regions.
- AI-assisted suggestions for floor regions on facade images and apartment regions on floor plans, with developer correction and record assignment.
- Apartment availability states: available, reserved and sold. Available apartment regions have no status fill; reserved regions have translucent yellow fill; sold regions have translucent red fill.
- Apartment details, finishing choices, favourites and comparison across developments and developers.
- Natural-language buyer preferences used to select actual properties in the platform catalogue and explain matches.
- Development infrastructure maps, including schools, kindergartens, shops, shopping centres and parks.
- Optional 3D site scenes, optional linked 360-degree panoramas and optional apartment 3D scenes. Missing material hides its corresponding section or action.
- Developer management of its own developments, buildings, floors, apartments, parking, images, descriptions, availability and enquiries.
- Internal apartment and parking enquiries with automatically attached object context. Public manager telephone numbers are not displayed.
- An enquiry does not perform a purchase, payment or automatic reservation.
- Parking selection is limited to available spaces and opens an enquiry form; a separate purchase or reservation workflow is not required.

Exact checkpoints, resource limits and deployment parameters remain implementation/evaluation decisions.
